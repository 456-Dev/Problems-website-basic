About Question The Day

One question.
About twenty-five strangers.
Almost no filtering.

A street-interview series by Michael — Big Nose Michael. Every episode he picks one question, walks up to strangers, asks every one of them the same thing, and keeps nearly every answer.

Why

It isn’t a poll.

Nobody is counted, ranked or graded here, and no answer is the right one. The point is range: how many different directions twenty-five people take the same few words — the joke, the confession, the lecture, the shrug.

Some questions are huge (What is reality?), some are about where we were standing (What do you think about tourists?), some are just fun (What is your least favorite number?). Curiosity is the only rule.

How an episode happens
  1. 1

    A question

    Timely, silly, or enormous. One per episode, asked word for word to everyone.

  2. 2

    The street

    Mostly New York, and a trip through thirty-odd cities — Manila, Cape Town, Budapest, Rio. Whoever stops, answers.

  3. 3

    The cut

    Nearly every answer stays in. A minute or two, a whole street of opinions.

  4. 4

    Posted

    On YouTube and Instagram as @bignosemichael, and every one lives here with its own page.
